Paperknight

Offline chess training for e-ink readers: rated puzzles, Lichess studies, your own opening repertoire with spaced repetition, an analysis board and Stockfish, on the e-ink screen and without a network connection.

Install Source on GitHub

What it does

  • Puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database, fully offline: a Glicko-2 rating, puzzles around your level, themes, hints and solutions, missed puzzles back after 3, 7 and 21 days, and stats per theme.
  • Studies: any PGN or Lichess study, with variations, comments, arrows, interactive lessons, and a move list you can tap.
  • Openings: drill your own repertoire with spaced repetition. New moves first, tested on the days after; the moves you know are played for you.
  • Analysis board with Stockfish's evaluation and best line; save a line as a study.
  • Play against Stockfish at the strength you choose, or two players face to face on one Kindle.
  • Made for e-ink: moves that don't flash, a night mode, opening names, links shown as QR codes.

Everything runs on the device: no account, no tracking, no network needed once installed.

Install

You need a jailbroken Kindle with KOReader (tested on a Kindle Paperwhite 2). In a terminal on the Kindle (kterm or SSH):

curl -fkL https://github.com/corentingi/paperknight/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

The installer shows what it will install: the plugin, the puzzle pack, an emoji font, and optionally Stockfish 18 built for the Paperwhite 2 (about 3 MB), the engine behind the analysis board and playing against the engine. It checks every download before changing anything. Then restart KOReader: Paperknight is in the Tools menu. Run it again to update: it downloads only what changed, and your own studies are kept.

Without a terminal

Download paperknight-<version>-usb.zip from the latest release, unzip it and copy its koreader folder onto the Kindle over USB.

Your studies and repertoires

Copy PGN files (a Lichess study exported as PGN, for example) to koreader/paperknight/studies/ or koreader/paperknight/repertoires/ on the Kindle.
