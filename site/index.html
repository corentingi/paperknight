KOReader plugin · made for the Kindle Paperwhite

Paperknight

Offline chess training for e-ink readers: rated puzzles, Lichess studies, your own opening repertoire with spaced repetition, an analysis board and Stockfish, on the e-ink screen and without a network connection.

Install Source on GitHub

What it does

  • Puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database, fully offline: a Glicko-2 rating, puzzles around your level, themes, hints and solutions, missed puzzles back after 3, 7 and 21 days, and stats per theme.
  • Studies: any PGN or Lichess study, with variations, comments, arrows, interactive lessons, and a move list you can tap.
  • Openings: drill your own repertoire with spaced repetition. New moves first, tested on the days after; the moves you know are played for you.
  • Analysis board with Stockfish's evaluation and best line; save a line as a study.
  • Play against Stockfish at the strength you choose, or two players face to face on one Kindle.
  • Master games: guess the moves of 75 famous games, from the Immortal Game to Ding – Gukesh; good alternatives count, key moments are announced.
  • Made for e-ink: moves that don't flash, a night mode, opening names, links shown as QR codes.

Everything runs on the device: no account, no tracking, no network needed once installed.

Install

You need an e-reader with KOReader: a jailbroken Kindle (tested on a Kindle Paperwhite 2), a Kobo, a PocketBook… Only the Paperwhite 2 has been tested: on another one, say how it went.

Over USB

Download paperknight-<version>-usb.zip from the latest release and unzip it. Connect the Kindle and copy what is inside the zip's koreader folder (plugins, fonts, paperknight) into the device's KOReader folder (koreader on a Kindle, .adds/koreader on a Kobo, applications/koreader on a PocketBook); don't replace that folder itself (on a Mac, Finder would delete KOReader's own files). Restart KOReader: Paperknight is in the Tools menu. The zip holds everything, Stockfish 18 for the Paperwhite 2 included (the engine behind the analysis board and playing against the engine).

In a terminal

On the e-reader (kterm or SSH; the installer finds KOReader on a Kindle, a Kobo or a PocketBook):

curl -fkL https://github.com/corentingi/paperknight/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

The installer shows what it will install and offers Stockfish. It checks every download before changing anything, and your own studies are kept.

Updates

In Paperknight, Settings… → Check for updates… (Wi-Fi on): it downloads only what changed. It checks GitHub's certificate with KOReader's own certificate list, then every archive against the checksum written in the installer; a device whose curl is too old to check the certificate downloads without that check, and says so. The terminal command uses curl -k so that it works everywhere.

Your studies and repertoires

Copy PGN files (a Lichess study exported as PGN, for example) to koreader/paperknight/studies/ or koreader/paperknight/repertoires/ on the Kindle.
